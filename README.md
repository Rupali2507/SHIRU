# SHIRU — AI-Powered Agentic Commerce

> **Your personal AI buyer that understands what you want, finds the right products, and helps you buy them.**

SHIRU is an AI-powered personal shopping assistant built for **agentic commerce**.

Instead of making users browse through hundreds of products, compare prices, and manually complete every step, SHIRU lets users simply **tell it what they want** — through voice or text.

SHIRU understands the request, searches merchant catalogs, compares products, recommends the best option, and can initiate a secure checkout through **Razorpay**.

At the same time, SHIRU provides merchants with an AI-ready storefront that makes their products **discoverable and transactable by AI buyers**.

---

## ✨ What is SHIRU?

SHIRU connects two sides of commerce:

### 🛍️ AI Buyer

Users can interact with SHIRU naturally:

> "Find me comfortable black running shoes under ₹4,000."

or simply speak:

> 🎙️ "I need running shoes for daily jogging, preferably black and under four thousand."

SHIRU can then:

- Understand the user's intent
- Search available products
- Filter by requirements such as price, category, color, size, etc.
- Compare relevant products
- Recommend the best match
- Explain why it recommends it
- Ask for confirmation before purchasing
- Create an order
- Open Razorpay checkout

---

### 🏪 AI-Transactable Merchant

Merchants can:

- Create their store
- Add products
- Upload product images
- Add pricing and stock
- Define sizes and colors
- Add AI metadata
- Make products discoverable by SHIRU
- Receive and manage orders

This allows merchants to become **AI-commerce ready** instead of relying only on traditional storefront browsing.

---

# 🎯 Problem

Traditional online shopping requires users to:

1. Search for a product
2. Open multiple listings
3. Compare prices
4. Compare features
5. Check availability
6. Decide what to buy
7. Add to cart
8. Enter checkout details
9. Complete payment

This creates friction, especially when users already know what they need but don't want to spend time searching.

At the same time, merchants need a way to make their products understandable and accessible to **AI shopping agents**.
